Step 2 - Embeddings and index in ChromaDB

Model: sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dims, cosine, normalized embeddings)
Index: ChromaDB PersistentClient, collection "docs", one entry per chunk
       (id = chunk_id from step 1, document = text, metadata = source, doc_title,
        section, chunk_index, split, char_count)

  chunks indexed: 468
  chunks over the model's 256-token limit (truncated at embedding): 134
  full rebuild from scratch: same counts and same retrieval results
  retrieval (6 hand-made queries): hit@1 2/6, hit@3 4/6

Configuration B (experiment, 800/100, scratch only, not delivered):
  chunks indexed: 613 (avg 599 chars)
  chunks over the token limit: 23
  retrieval: hit@1 2/6, hit@3 4/6 (same queries, same results)

Notes:
